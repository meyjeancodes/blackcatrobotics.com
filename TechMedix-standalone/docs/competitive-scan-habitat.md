## The category
"Construction 3D printing" (c3Dp / 3DCP) = using 3D printing to fabricate buildings or
components. Methods: extrusion (concrete/cement), powder bonding, additive welding.
Done on-site (gantry, robotic arm, autonomous vehicle) or off-site.
Known field gaps (Wikipedia): material-mix optimization, process consistency / quality
control, structural durability, and regulation/standardization lag. [VERIFIED]
