The Full Loop

From telemetry
to verified repair.

TechMedix closes the loop that most maintenance tools leave open. Every alert becomes a diagnosed problem, a recommended fix, a dispatched technician, and a verified repair — with everything learned fed back into the system.

01
Detect
Continuous telemetry monitoring across every robot. TechMedix flags abnormal behavior the moment it deviates from baseline — not after the breakdown.
02
Diagnose
Every alert is matched against documented failure modes for that platform — the same knowledge base our technicians use. TechMedix explains why it thinks something will fail, not just that it might.
03
Predict
Failures flagged up to 48 hours before they happen, with a predicted failure window and fleet-level impact estimate. You get time to act — not a surprise.
04
Repair
TechMedix recommends the exact part and connects you with a certified technician. Parts are mapped to documented failure modes — replace the right component before it breaks, not after.
05
Verify
After the repair, TechMedix confirms the fix worked — telemetry returns to normal, the alert closes, and the outcome is logged. The system gets smarter with every verified repair.
