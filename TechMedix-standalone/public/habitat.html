HABITAT.
Design your home by conversation.

HABITAT is the AI home-construction division of BlackCat Robotics. Describe your ideal home in plain language — our AI extracts every detail, generates a floor plan, and produces a real, line-item quote. Lock the build with a deposit and we take it from there.

What it does

Construction, automated end to end.

Autonomous robotics transformed warehouses, farms, and factories. Housing hasn't followed — until now. HABITAT targets the full chain: AI-driven design, off-site robotic fabrication, and on-site assembly coordinated by a single intelligence layer.

Conversational design

Describe your home in words. HABITAT AI extracts bedrooms, baths, footprint, style, and features — no architectural software required.

Instant floor plan

A parametric 2D layout is generated from your brief, with live design-rule checks (square-footage, off-grid requirements, structural review).

Real quote

Line-item pricing for construction, solar, rainwater, and permits — plus a 10% deposit to lock your design. No guessing.

Robotic build

Off-site mills and printers produce precision components; on-site systems assemble with minimal manual labor and tighter tolerances.

How it works

From idea to locked build in four steps.

01

Describe

Tell the AI what you want — "3 bed, off-grid, solar, modern." It captures the brief.

02

Generate

HABITAT produces a floor plan and a full quote you can tweak by chatting.

03

Save

Sign in and save your design. Return to it anytime from any device.

04

Lock

Place a 10% deposit through Stripe. Your design is reserved and our team reaches out within 24 hours.

The AI Designer is live at /habitat/design. Deposit checkout is powered by Stripe; final pricing is confirmed after a site survey.
